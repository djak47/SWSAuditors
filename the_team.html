The Team

A collective of seasoned professionals dedicated to financial excellence, regulatory compliance, and strategic growth.

Principal

Solomon Wassyihun FCCA, IFRS.Dip

Solomon is an audit expert and is well versed with the International Standards on Auditing as well as the International Financial Reporting Standards. Solomon has spent several years auditing organizations and companies in the profit and not for profit sectors and has extensive experience in audit of organizations in the, NGO, financial services and commercial sectors.

He has also gained extensive experience in internal control assessments, procurement and expenditure reviews, and compliance appraisals. He is responsible for quality assurance and ensures that the audit is done in line with the International Standards on Auditing and the International Financial Reporting Standards.

Due to his extensive experience, Solomon is the point person for independent determination findings and makes audit recommendations for unprecedented, complex issues and problems. He serves as a key resource for the rest of the team in resolving unprecedented, non-standard issues and problems including identifying implications for management decision-making.

Solomon is a Chartered Certified Accountant and a member of The Chartered Certified Accountants. He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Accounting from the Addis Ababa University Faculty of Business and Economics. He has extensive training on accounting standards as well as taxation and strategic management.

He is an active member of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants, working closely with members of the committees to promote the largest global body of professional accountants in Ethiopia.

Audit Manager

Zergaw Negussu ACCA

Zergaw holds a Bachelor of Commerce Degree in accounting from Addis Ababa University. He is a Chartered Certified Accountant and a member of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) U.K. He is a specialized and seasoned Tax consultant at the firm and consults highly in various tax matters ranging from Vat, Corporate Tax Customs and Excise etc.

His experience of over 10 years presents him as a vital resource to our clients. He is technically competent to deal with any complex tax issues within the country. He is a keen student of international tax and financial management issues. Part of his experience is derived from working for a leading firm Zergaw consult.

Audit Manager

Tesfaye Wolkema

Tesfaye is an expert specializing in audit, financial reporting and management and financial consulting. His wide professional experience combined with his excellent educational background make him a first class audit manager and financial consultant. He holds a BA Degree in Accounting and Finance from the Addis Ababa University.

Tesfaye has many years of professional experience working in the internal audit departments of Ethiopian Red Cross Society, where he worked as an internal audit manager. During his time as an auditor manager of our firm, Tesfaye has leaded big audit assignments of large companies and of large nongovernmental organization.

In addition Tesfaye has adequate experience as a financial controller in the hotel, manufacturing and health industries where he got extensive experience in the fields of financial management, internal auditing, project appraisal, turning businesses around, managing human capital, business valuations etc.

During his long career Tesfaye was heavily involved in various tax planning structures and tax compliance work. He possesses excellent knowledge of the Ethiopian Tax system and extensive international tax knowledge.

Tesfaye is described by his colleagues as an intelligent accountant capable of delivering first class audit services and financial management solutions to businesses. He is hard working, enjoys teamwork and always keen to listen and analyze clients’ needs in order to give solutions to their business problems.
